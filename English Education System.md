## Standards and Regulations

All schools in England need to follow the [National curriculum](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-curriculum).

## Timeline

- Primary school: 4-11 yo
- Secondary school:  11-16 yo
	- You graduate by taking [GCSE](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/zj9746f#zqk29ty)s. You choose which subjects you want to take a GCSE at, but some subjects are mandatory, such as English and Maths. Most students take 8-10 subjects.
- Further education: 16-18 yo: You need to stay in educationuntil you are 18, but the choice is up to you.
	- [A-Levels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-level_(United_Kingdom)): Stay in school for 2 years. This is what people pick if they want to go to university afterwards.
	- [T-Levels](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-of-t-levels/introduction-of-t-levels?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Broadly equivalent in size to three A Levels and include substantial industry experience.
	- Apprenticeship.
	- Do some kind of vocational course, like a BTEC, at a community college.
	- ...
- Higher education: University.

## Key stages & Assessments

References:
- [The National Curriculum](https://www.gov.uk/national-curriculum)

Key stages:
- KS1 (Primary): 5-7 yo
	- Foundational skills in numeracy and literacy, such as basic reading, writing, and maths.
- KS2 (Primary): 7-11 yo
	- Science, art, PE, geography, history, and music. Critical thinking and problem-solving.
- KS3 (Secondary): 11-14 yo
	- Deepening knowledge in existing subjects. Foreign languages, technology, and computing.
- KS4 (Secondary): 14-16 yo
	- Allows students to tailor their education to their interests and further aspirations. This is where they choose which subjects they want to take a GCSE at.

Assessments:
- Year 1 (5-6 yo):
	- Reception Baseline Assessment
	- Early Years Foundation Stage Profile
	- [Phonic screening](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phonics-screening-check-information-for-parents)
- Year 2 (6-7 yo):
	- KS1 SATs (optional)
- Year 4 (8-9 yo):
	- Multiplication Tables Check
- Year 6 (10-11 yo):
	- KS2 SATs
- Year 10 (14-15 yo):
	- Some students take GCSEs early
- Year 11 (15-16 yo):
	- GCSEs
