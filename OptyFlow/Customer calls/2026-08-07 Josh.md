	- Ability to set something into the same "pattern type". E.g. put them all into "Saturday 3PM pattern". Not changing the actual day plans, but "put this into whatever would've run at Saturday 3PM", "I want this corridor to behave like Saturday"
	- Maybe the ability to "stop time", but this is a niche requirement. This seems similar to our "control mode" in the status list and setting the intersections into manual
- Status
	- Ped calls could be useful in the status list because they could break the coordination. But you don't want to display all ped calls.
	- Maybe display active phase plan, overlap plan, detector plan (but it may add noise). It is not that critical.
- Coordination configuration
	- Just having the values in the list is enough. We don't need to color the outliers yellow.
		- Maybe a button that says "highlight differences"
	- Firmware version would be useful
- Corridor health
	- We should display detector faults and alarms that could be relevant to the corridor
	- Historical data
		- Split history would be useful. But it is 3-dimensional (per intersection, per phase, per cycle)
- Time-Space Diagram
	- Different people prefer different orientation (horizontal vs vertical)

	- Why not merge:
		- Corridor management gives you ordering
		- Corridor management gives you time-space diagram
	- Why merge
		- Users may want to see information about a group of intersections that are not neccessarily part of a corridor
